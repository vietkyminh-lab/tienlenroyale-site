Delete Your Account & Data

Tiến Lên Royale, published by Royale Dragon Studio

You can delete your Tiến Lên Royale account and its data at any time. There are two ways, and neither requires an email address, a phone number, or any sign-in.

1. In the game (recommended)

Open Settings ▸ Account ▸ Delete account… and confirm. This permanently deletes your profile — chips, gems, level, statistics, and everything you own in the game — from your device, and, if your account has ever connected to our game server, the server's copy as well. Nothing is archived and nothing can be recovered afterwards.

If you don't see this option, your app version predates it — update the game, or use the request route below.

2. By request (if you've uninstalled the game)

Email tienlenroyal@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion request". If you know your player ID (shown as MY ID on the Social page in the game), include it so we can find your account; if you no longer have it, tell us the display name you used and roughly when you last played. We process deletion requests within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: display name, avatar, chip and gem balances, level, experience, statistics, and owned items
  • Your account record on our game server (if one exists), including every backup generation of it
  • Your device's pairing credentials with the server
  • Any sign-in links (for example a linked Google account, once sign-in ships)

What is retained, and why

  • Shared club records. If you played in a Royal Club, entries naming your random player ID may remain in other players' club history and table ledgers — these are the other members' financial record of their own club, and once your account is deleted the ID in them no longer refers to any account. They contain no personal information.
  • Analytics already collected. Pseudonymous Firebase analytics and crash data is retained and deleted on Google's standard schedules, as described in our privacy policy. It is not tied to your game account.

Deleting the app from your device without the steps above deletes the local save on that device, but not any server copy — use one of the two routes if your account ever played online.
